# Privacy Policy for Roll

**Effective date:** October 7, 2026
**Developer:** Avikalabs
**Contact:** avikalabs@gmail.com

Roll is a wallpaper app for Android that rotates your own photos on your home screen and lock screen. This policy explains what Roll does, and does not do, with your information.

## The short version

Roll does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. Roll has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access. Everything stays on your device.

## Information we collect

**None.** Roll does not collect any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location, contacts or any other data. Nothing is sent to Avikalabs or to any third party.

Roll does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

## Your photos and folders

Roll only accesses the photos and folders that **you** choose, using Android's system photo picker and folder picker. Roll uses them only to display them as your wallpaper, in the app and in the Roll widget and live wallpaper.

- Roll does not upload, copy to any server, analyze or share your photos.
- Roll keeps permission to read the photos and folders you selected so it can keep rotating them, even after a restart.
- If you add a folder, Roll checks that folder from time to time on your device for new photos to add to your roll.
- You can remove any photo from your roll at any time. You can also revoke Roll's access in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Information stored on your device

To work, Roll saves the following **only on your device**:

- the list of photos and folders in your roll, and their order;
- your timer settings, playback order and screen choices (home or lock);
- app preferences such as theme and double-tap settings.

This information never leaves your device through Roll. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Device backups

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include Roll's app preferences, such as theme and double-tap settings, in your device backup to your Google account. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by Avikalabs. Your roll (the list of photos) is not included in backups.

## Permissions Roll uses

- **Set wallpaper:** to change your home and lock screen wallpaper.
- **Run at startup:** to keep your wallpaper rotation going after your phone restarts.
- **Photo and folder access:** only for the items you pick, granted through Android's system pickers.

## Third-party services

Roll does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Roll is distributed through **Google Play**. If Roll offers optional in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Google Play. Avikalabs never receives your payment details. Google's handling of your information is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children's privacy

Roll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Because Roll does not collect or transmit your data, your photos and settings remain protected by your device's own security.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
