# Privacy Policy for Renewly

**Publisher:** Avikalabs
**Effective date:** October 9, 2026

Renewly is a subscription tracker for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter into the app.

## The short version

Renewly does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no bank connections.

## Information you enter

You may enter details such as subscription names, amounts, billing cycles, charge dates, categories, private notes, and an optional first name used for the home-screen greeting. This information is stored only in a local database on your device. Avikalabs never receives it and cannot see it.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** — to show renewal reminders before a subscription charges. You can turn this off at any time in Android settings.
- **Run at startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`)** — to reschedule your reminders after your phone restarts or the time zone changes.

Renewly does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

## Exporting and importing

If you choose to export your subscriptions (JSON or CSV), the data is copied to your device's clipboard so you can paste it wherever you like. What happens after that is under your control. Importing reads only the text you paste into the app.

## Third parties

Renewly contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

## Backups

The app's data is not included in Android cloud backups. If you uninstall the app or change phones, use the export feature first to keep a copy.

## Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time from **Settings → Delete all data**. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

Renewly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
